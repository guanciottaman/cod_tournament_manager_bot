# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 7, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and stores information.

By using the Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

The Bot only collects the following information:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Guild (Server) IDs
* Discord Channel IDs (when necessary for the Bot's functionality)
* Discord Role IDs (when necessary for the Bot's functionality)

No personal information such as usernames, email addresses, passwords, messages, or IP addresses is collected.

## Why We Collect This Information

The collected data is used solely to provide the Bot's features and functionality, including:

* Managing events
* Assigning roles
* Tracking participation
* Storing configuration required for the Bot to operate correctly

The Bot does not use collected data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose unrelated to its functionality.

## Data Retention

Data is kept only for as long as it is necessary to provide the relevant functionality.

Event-related data is deleted once it is no longer required for that event or feature.

## Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell, rent, or share collected data with third parties unless required by law.

## Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored data against unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion.
